Client Details

Your own clients, from your e-Partner Policy Listing. Nobody else sees this page's data — it's filed under your sign-in only.

Where does this file come from?
  1. On your computer, sign in to e-Partner and open Online Enquiry → Policy Enquiry → View all policies → Submit.
  2. Click your GE Refresh bookmark. It reads every policy and downloads a file named portfolio_update_<date>.json.
  3. Come back here, tap Import update file above, and choose that file. It uploads to this page only — nobody else can see it.
