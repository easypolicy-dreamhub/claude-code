# DISC (step 1): working-style questionnaire for candidates

Tables: `private/schema_phase7.sql` (`disc_invites`, `disc_results`). Content generated by `tools/gen_disc.py` (28 original questions, English + Chinese).

## Flow
1. A candidate with a finished Career Code of Strong / Good / Borderline fit shows **Ready to invite** on the candidate list and page (advisor dashboard only; the advisor email is unchanged). "Not a typical fit" never gets the button.
2. The advisor chooses **Send invite by email**, **Create link to copy** (WhatsApp), or **Start DISC now** (in the room, one-time link, 24 hours, no digits check).
3. The candidate opens `/career-code/disc/?t=<code>`, confirms the last 4 digits of their mobile (not needed for in-the-room links), ticks consent, answers 28 groups (pick the word MOST like them and LEAST like them), and sees their style with positive text. The result is emailed to them if they have an email address.
4. Advisor and uplines get a link-only email. The advisor sees scores, levels, how to talk to them, pressure, role fit, interview questions, answer-pattern flags and a comparison with the Career Code.

## Rules
- Link valid 14 days; works once; 5 wrong digit attempts lock it. After 3 days with no result the tag becomes "DISC not done after 3 days" and the advisor can **Send reminder** (same link). After 14 days: **Re-invite** (new link, old one dead). Finished: **Invite to redo**, newest result is shown.
- Tags: Ready to invite, DISC invited, DISC not done after 3 days, DISC link expired, DISC done. Separate from the follow-up status.
- Not suitable / Joined switch off invite buttons and reminders (server refuses too). Sending an invite while the status is New asks "Mark as Contacted?". Finishing DISC never changes the status.
- Results and emails go to whoever owns the candidate when they finish. The advisor who ran an in-the-room session is not emailed.
- Scoring is server-side: most +1, least -1 per style (each -28..28, always adding to 0). Which word belongs to which style is only in `private/data/disc.json`.
- Deleting a candidate deletes their DISC invites and results.
- Not in step 1: resume upload and the "Ready to invite" list on Team Activity (step 2).

Tests: `php tests/phase7.php`, `node tests/e2e_disc.js`.
